# Preliminary brand research

Date: 2026-08-13

## Scope and caveat

This is an initial public-web screening, not formal trademark clearance or legal advice. The USPTO advises searching its trademark database and similar marks as part of a clearance search. WIPO states that its Global Brand Database covers international and participating national/regional collections, but recommends also checking national or regional registers and consulting a trademark attorney.

## Candidate screening

- `PageMint` was rejected because Microsoft Store lists an existing product named “PageMint: PDF Merge, Split & Rotate” by SinYus.
- `PageForge` was rejected because public search results show “PDF Page Forge” and a separate PageForge SEO product.
- `PaperMosaic` is the current working candidate. Exact-product searches returned no obvious PDF/software product under that exact compound name; search results were mainly generic paper-mosaic art and unrelated mosaic software. This does not establish trademark availability.
